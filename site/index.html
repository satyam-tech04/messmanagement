Support

Most questions are fastest answered by your mess office — they run your account, your plan and the counter. For anything about the app itself, write to support@mealadda.app. Include your mess name and the mobile number or email you sign in with.

I can't sign in.

Students sign in with the mobile number registered with their mess; staff and administrators use their email address. Your first password is given to you by your mess, and you will be asked to choose your own the first time you sign in. If you have forgotten it, your mess office can reset it.

My QR code says I can't be served.

The code shows why — for example no running plan, a meal you marked yourself out of, or a meal outside its serving time. Plans and payments are managed by your mess, so speak to the mess office. Counter staff can also record your meal manually.

I think I was charged or marked incorrectly.

Every plan, payment, absence and served meal is recorded with a date and time. Your mess office can look up the exact record and correct it.

Can I use a screenshot of my QR code?

No. The code refreshes itself while it is on screen, each one expires within seconds, and it is checked by the server — so a screenshot or a code forwarded to someone else will be refused at the counter.
