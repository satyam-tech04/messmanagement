Delete your account

How to have your MealAdda account and personal data deleted, what is removed, and what may be kept.

On this page

  1. Request deletion
  2. What is deleted
  3. What may be kept
  4. Delete only some data

Request deletion

MealAdda accounts are created and managed by your hostel or mess, so deletion is handled together with them. You can use either route below.

  1. Ask your mess office. Tell them you want your MealAdda account deleted. They will close your account and pass the request to us.
  2. Or email us directly at support@mealadda.app with the subject “Delete my MealAdda account”, and include:
    • your full name;
    • the name of your hostel or mess;
    • the mobile number or email address you sign in with; and
    • your roll number, if you have one.
  3. We confirm it is you. To stop anyone deleting someone else’s account, we check the request against your mess’s records and may contact you on your registered number or email.
  4. Your account is deleted within 30 days of confirmation, and we reply to let you know it is done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and password, so you can no longer sign in.
  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email address, room number and roll number.
  • Your profile photograph.
  • Your meal ratings, comments and any photos you attached to them.
  • Sessions and data stored by the app on your device, once you uninstall it.

What may be kept

Your mess may need to keep a record of what it served and what was paid, to keep its own accounts accurate and to meet legal and tax obligations. Where it does:

  • Attendance, plan, payment and bill records may be kept for as long as your mess needs them or the law requires, but are no longer linked to your name or contact details.
  • Audit and security logs may be kept for a limited period to protect the Service against fraud and misuse.

Anything kept is deleted once it is no longer needed. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Delete only some data

You do not have to close your account to remove some information. You can ask your mess office, or email us, to remove your profile photograph, delete a meal rating or photo you submitted, or correct your details — and keep using MealAdda.
